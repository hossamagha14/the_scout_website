The Scout Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Scout collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app.

Information we collect

  • Account information (email, sign-in provider, username, role).
  • Profile information you provide (bio, age, position, preferred foot, profile photo, phone number if added).
  • Content and activity data (uploaded videos, comments, likes, saves, follows, scouting actions, notifications).
  • Device and technical data (push notification tokens, basic diagnostics/crash logs from platform services).

How we use information

  • Provide core app features (feed, profile, social interactions, sharing).
  • Personalize feed ordering and discovery signals.
  • Send in-app and push notifications.
  • Protect platform security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.

Third-party services

The app uses Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Functions, Hosting, Messaging) and platform sign-in providers (Google/Apple where enabled).

Data retention

We retain your account data while your account is active. If you request account deletion in-app, we permanently delete your account data used by app features, including authentication account, profile, uploaded media references, and linked social/activity records associated with the app workflow.

Limited operational records (for example, security and billing logs) may be retained where legally required.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from the app: Profile - Delete account. Deletion is intended to be permanent.

Children

The Scout is not intended for users below the minimum legal age in applicable jurisdictions without required consent.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@thescout.app
