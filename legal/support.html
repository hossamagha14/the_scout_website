The Scout Support

Email: support@thescout.app

Response target: 2 to 5 business days

Account deletion

In-app path: Profile, then Delete account. This permanently removes app account data used by core features. Some legally required operational records may be retained.

What to include when contacting support

  • Your account email (if applicable).
  • Device model and OS version.
  • App version/build number.
  • Short description and screenshot/screen recording if possible.
