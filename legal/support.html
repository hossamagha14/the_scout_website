The Scout Support

Contact email: thescout.eg@outlook.com

Use this address for technical support, account help, and general inquiries.

Typical response time: 2–5 business days.

Self-service in the app

  • Legal documents: Privacy Policy · Terms & Conditions (also linked from Profile → menu).
  • Account deletion: Profile → menu (⋮) → Delete account. This is intended to permanently remove your account and core app data as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Safety: report videos or comments and block users from the flows provided in the feed and moderation screens; manage blocked accounts from Profile where available.

What to include in your email

  • The email address associated with your account (if any).
  • Device model and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18).
  • App version or build if you know it.
  • A short description of the issue, steps to reproduce, and screenshots or a screen recording if possible.

Privacy-specific requests

For privacy questions or data requests, use the same address: thescout.eg@outlook.com — please put “Privacy” in the subject line.
