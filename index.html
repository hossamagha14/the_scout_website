Our vision

The Scout connects talent and clubs directly—your best moments in front of people who can actually move your career, with no one in the middle. When more players across Egypt are scouted on merit, the whole game sharpens—from local pitches to the national team. We are here so the dream of playing at the highest level stays honest, and so Egypt can chase its football glory again with pride.

What’s in the app

A closer look at what actually lives inside The Scout—built for players and clubs who live on the ball.

Full-screen video feed

Scroll football the way you already watch the world—full screen, sound on, next clip ready when yours is done. When someone earns a second look, hop straight into their profile without losing the rhythm.

Player profiles & media

Your saves, your posts, your scouting list—laid out so a curious eye can move from one moment to the next without digging through menus. The story stays on the tape, not in a spreadsheet.

Search & discovery

Find people the way you think about the game—by name, role, or form—then slip back into the feed when you have found your mark. Discovery should feel like a conversation on the touchline, not a database query.

Scouting & rankings

Keep a shortlist of players you are quietly tracking and watch how the room reacts when their clips start circulating. Rankings follow attention—so form shows up where it matters.

Shareable moments

When a clip deserves a wider audience, send it out in a tap. Saves, reshares, and word-of-mouth all stay inside one football-shaped loop—no detours, no second-hand retelling of what you already filmed.

Clubs on the same tape

Scouts and staff watch exactly what players post—same frame, same touch, same pause for replay. Decisions start from the moment everyone actually saw, not from who heard what through a chain of phones.

Your pitch in one place

Foot, position, bio, highlights—the details you would shout across a warm-up now sit beside your best clips. One place to introduce yourself before the first whistle even belongs to you.

From training ground to spotlight

The Tuesday-night stepover matters here too. The Scout is for the small sessions that quietly sharpen your touch—because confidence on the ball is built long before anyone shouts your name from the stands.

Two entry points, one product

Same dark theme and neon accent; flows tuned to the role you pick.

Talent

Onboard with the board metaphor, sign in, complete profile setup if required, then land on the home feed. Your content and stats live under one profile.

Club

A dedicated club path so scouts and staff watch the same moments players post—one place to discover, shortlist, and follow up without the story getting lost.
