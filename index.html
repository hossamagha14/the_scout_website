Get the app What it does

The pitch, reimagined as a board.

The Scout takes its visual language from the substitution board: dark surfaces, neon green accent, a tiled “S” pattern, and type that feels stamped for match day. The app is a place to post your best plays, build a profile, and be seen by scouts, clubs, and other players—on mobile, with Firebase at the core.

Whether you are chasing your next step as a talent or evaluating form as a club user, the same feed-first experience keeps the focus on video and identity.

What’s in the app

A product snapshot—mapped to the current Flutter app structure, not a roadmap promise.

Full-screen video feed

Vertical, TikTok-style feed of football clips, with profile access and share links for public videos.

Player profiles & media

Grids of your videos, saved posts, and scouted players; talent setup when your profile needs completion.

Search & discovery

Find players in the community with filters aligned to the product’s feed.

Scouting & rankings

Track who you are watching and how talent stacks in competitive lists.

Social graph

Follow other players; notifications when there is something to see.

Sign-in & roles

Google and Apple where enabled; club flows sit alongside the main talent path.

Safety & moderation

Reporting, blocking, and video moderation tools to keep the pitch clean.

Push & deep links

Open shared clips from notifications with the same in-app experience.

Two entry points, one product

Same dark theme and neon accent; flows tuned to the role you pick.

Talent

Onboard with the board metaphor, sign in, complete profile setup if required, then land on the home feed. Your content and stats live under one profile.

Club

Dedicated club sign-in path for organizations that use the same infrastructure—so evaluation and content stay in the same universe as player-submitted video.
